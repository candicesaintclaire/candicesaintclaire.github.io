Not sure what you have or what to do with it? I've been there.

I encourage you to reach out — I'm happy to talk, answer a few questions, or just help you think through next steps. No cost, no obligation, and no pressure. We can connect by email, direct message, or phone, whatever works best for you.

I approach every outreach from a research orientation rather than a sales orientation. If you've recently inherited, discovered, or acquired something you think might be historically or culturally significant — or if you simply have a question — this is a good place to start.
